# higgsfield brief, the remaining world assets

third of three briefs (globe-higgsfield-brief.md, creature-higgsfield-brief.md
cover the leads). these are the supporting world-imagery assets. same house
rules for all: candidates in batches of 4 to 6, two-strikes law, proof every
candidate in place on the real surface before presenting, CSS versions stay
as the forever-fallback.

## 1. the misted veil (the tear)

the wall of the room thinning into fog where the world shows through. ruled:
NO hard edge, the wall dissolves.

> a porcelain white wall softly dissolving into luminous fog at its center,
> the fog thins to reveal a faint warm glow beyond, no hard edges anywhere,
> gradient of white to pale grey mist, tiny drifting particles of light,
> soft upper-left light, minimal, dreamlike but calm, no text

- deliverable: one loop (fog breathing, 8s) + one still, portrait.
- constraint: must sit INSIDE a porcelain room without reading as a hole or
  a portal ring. it is weather, not a doorway frame.

## 2. the little beings (other agents in the world)

tiny creatures with faces seen at distance, trust deepens their ink.

> a scattered group of tiny handcrafted creatures on a soft white void, each
> a simple rounded ink-and-porcelain form with two small dark eyes, varying
> silhouettes but one family, some faint like pale grey ghosts and some
> fully inked, standing at different distances, soft upper-left light,
> minimal, no text

- deliverable: a sheet of 8 to 12 distinct silhouettes (stills), plus one
  4s idle loop of three of them.
- constraint: monochrome, they must read at 20 to 40px, none may upstage
  the pebble or the inkling.

## 3. the app icon (eclipse globe)

the ruled icon treatment: the eclipse, a porcelain sphere with a dark moon
crossing it. flat enough for iOS masking.

> a minimal app icon, a matte porcelain white sphere with a dark charcoal
> circle eclipsing its upper right, soft studio light upper left, subtle
> subsurface glow, centered on a flat porcelain background, no gloss, no
> text, geometric, calm

- deliverable: 1024x1024 masters, one light ground and one night ground.
- constraint: no grid lines, no stars, reads at 60px on a home screen.

## 4. the night habitat ground

the dark room the companion originally lived in (Night surface). kept for
night mode and for the away room's mood if keem rules it back in.

> the interior of a vast quiet dark room, deep charcoal walls fading to
> black, a soft pool of cool light on a smooth floor at center, faint mist
> at the edges, minimal, serene, cinematic but empty, no furniture, no
> windows, no text

- deliverable: one still, phone-portrait, plus a 10s loop with the light
  pool breathing.
- constraint: must hold type at 500 weight in porcelain white on top.

## 5. the wax seal ✦ (the sealed word)

the seal mark used wherever something waits unopened.

> a small ink-black wax seal stamped with a four-pointed star, pressed into
> matte white paper, slight relief and sheen on the wax, macro photography,
> soft upper-left light, minimal, no text

- deliverable: one still with alpha, plus a 2s press-and-set loop.
- constraint: monochrome, reads at 24px.

## not in this brief

- the globe and the creature family (their own briefs).
- the P wordmark and logo (drawn geometry, never generated).
- ui chrome of any kind (buttons, docks, sheets are code, not imagery).
