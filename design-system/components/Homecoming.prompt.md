The homecoming is the product. Findings read like a field journal and every claim carries a checkable source. Nothing renders open by default — the SealedNote waits until the human opens it. Verbs are Keep / Later, never accept/dismiss.

```jsx
<SealedNote by="Sol" count={3} onOpen={...} />
<Finding sources={[{ label: 'lumen · archive' }, { label: 'p.14' }]}>
  One paper argues the opposite of what we believe. I kept its strongest point.
</Finding>
```
