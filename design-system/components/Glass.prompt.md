Surfaces. App chrome is opaque paper: Sheet (reading and typing) and Menu (glancing) are white cards with hairline rings, no blur. Glass exists for ONE job: lensing over world imagery (the tear, the globe, world photos), where its blur has something alive to work on. Never stack glass on glass more than once, and never put glass on paper chrome.

```jsx
<Glass kind="element" style={{padding:'10px 14px'}}>floating over the tear</Glass>
<Sheet onClose={close}> …brief form… </Sheet>
<Menu top={102} right={14}> …roster rows… </Menu>
```
