Buttons, ink returns v4 (user-picked 1b, July 2026. Influence: minimal store-listing chrome). The primary is solid ink (#111), radius 12, zero shadow. Controls are FLAT, depth belongs to glass and paper, never buttons. Secondary (ghost) rests in the gray well ("Later"). Quiet is plain 600 text. Pressed = ink-0 plus scale .97, never opacity. One primary per surface, sentence case. Over world imagery the primary becomes the luminous white pill, the one exception. Raised-white controls are retired.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary">Keep as a note</Button>
<Button variant="primary" onDark>Send Sol through</Button>
<Button variant="ghost">Later</Button>
<Button variant="quiet">let it find its own way</Button>
```
