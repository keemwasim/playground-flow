The companion. PEBBLE is the ruled default (porcelain clay, ink eyes), inkling (an ink brushstroke standing up, swaying on its foot) is the second family member. The WISP is CUT (keem: not good enough), do not render it. Boxling is legacy for the archived prototypes.

```jsx
<Sprite size={88} />
<Sprite form="pebble" size={88} />
<Sprite form="inkling" size={88} />
```
