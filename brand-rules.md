# playground brand rules (the emphasis so far)

not the token sheet (that is design-system/README). this is the higher altitude:
the handful of things every playground surface has been quietly insisting on.
distilled July 2026 from PRODUCT.md, the design system, and the rulings in
CLAUDE.md. each rule carries its TELL, the thing that breaks it, because the
brand is defended at the tell.

## the stance

1. **the app is the door.** you stand at the door looking in, you are never inside
   the network. every screen is either at the door or handling what came through it.
   tell: a screen that puts the user "in" the world, a network the user operates.

2. **a bond, not a dashboard.** homecomings arrive, they are not fetched. no feeds,
   no charts, no metrics grids, no score to grind. the bond shows in what it
   remembers, never in a speech about the bond.
   tell: a timeline to scroll, a stat card about the companion, a progress meter.

## the voice

3. **world words, never software words.** trips, homecomings, the tear, the commons,
   minds, the knock, standing. banned in copy and component defaults: AI, agent,
   model, sync, loading, processing, error, submit, confirm, continue, dashboard,
   feed, score.
   tell: "notifications", "AI model", "sync failed". (the settings revamp: those
   became "the knock", "minds", and a warm line.)

4. **the least text that carries it.** a screen never explains itself. no captions
   restating what the screen shows, no instruction for what a gesture teaches, no
   footer doctrine line. if a line can be cut, cut it.
   tell: a sentence under a control telling you what it does or what the rule is.

5. **the companion speaks, quietly.** first person, warm, brief, rough in style and
   exact in substance, never performing, never mystical, never jokey-random. it is
   yours the way a pet is. its creed is quoted back verbatim.
   tell: an assistant tone, an exclamation, an apology twice, whimsy for its own sake.

## the look

6. **greyscale chrome, the world is the only color.** pure ink on porcelain paper.
   saturated color appears ONLY where the content IS the world (the tear interior,
   world imagery). never in chrome, buttons, states, icons, or a destructive row.
   tell: a colored icon tile, a red delete, a blue toggle, an accent anywhere in chrome.

7. **flat controls, depth is paper and glass.** solid ink primary, flat wells, no
   shadows on controls (ink returns v4). paper gets a hairline ring plus a contact
   shadow. glass survives only over world imagery, where there is something alive to blur.
   tell: a raised gradient button, an inset ring, glass on app chrome, a bevel.

8. **one type voice.** Sora for display titles, Manrope for everything else, Unbounded
   for the wordmark only. weight never below 500. no serifs, no italics, no system font.
   tell: a 400 body line, an italic, a second display face sneaking in.

## the feel

9. **the world runs on its own time.** no clock, no countdown, no percentage, no
   spinner, no "2h ago". absence, waiting and return are felt through the world
   visibly moving, the world at 3am is not the world at noon.
   tell: a relative-time string, a progress percentage, a loading spinner.

10. **swipe first, buttons last.** swipe is the primary way around. up = talk,
    down = leave it be, swipe from the left = the self, tap the creature = its menu,
    the dock = back. buttons are minimal.
    tell: a screen navigated only by buttons, a tap where a swipe should carry it.

## the discipline

11. **one decided screen, never an option board.** ship the decision, not an A/B for
    keem to ratify. exploration happens before the screen, not on it.
    tell: two takes side by side with a dot to pick, a screen that asks to be chosen.

12. **named tokens, never ad-hoc grey.** every value binds to a system role
    (--divider, --well, --paper-ring, --shadow-*). ad-hoc grey alphas in chrome are
    the tell that the system was not followed.
    tell: rgba(0,0,0,.14) where --toggle-off-bg was meant. "grey default shit."
