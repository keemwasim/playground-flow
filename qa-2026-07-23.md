## what was broken, and fixed in this pass

1. **the errand was never delivered.** the product promise (it hands you what
   you asked for) did not happen: beat 05 was a room with a creature in it.
   FIXED: it now comes home carrying the thing, shows a holding cue on the
   room, and hands it over in the talk in its own voice with what it answers
   underneath.
2. **the agent switcher silently failed.** tapping a row in the dock did not
   change rooms, the card's own toggle swallowed the click. FIXED.
3. **the away room was dead space.** no creature (correct), but nothing to
   look at and no sense of time passing. FIXED: the world it is inside now
   turns there, on its own clock, dimmed. no timer, no progress bar.

## what is still off, and deliberately not fixed here

- **the creature has no state over time.** no moods, no reaction to being
  ignored, no visible change from training. it is the same creature on day
  one and day fifty. this is the largest hole in the product and it needs a
  design decision, not a patch.
- **the world does not remember.** phases cycle on a timer rather than
  advancing from real elapsed time, so returning after three days looks
  identical to returning after three seconds.
- **replies are canned.** the talk simulates the harness rules correctly but
  the content is a fixed bank. real behaviour waits on the backend.
- **friends, settings, the store, marketplace, playground plus, inventory**
  are ghosted destinations with no design behind them yet.
- **the second agent is cosmetic.** it has its own face, room and name, but
  no separate conversation, standing or history.
