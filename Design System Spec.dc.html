Design System
v4 · July 2026 — hardening pass · light & minimal, paper & ink · built to PRODUCT.md
Ruled: the creature is the wisp, the tear is the misted veil, agent marks are little beings, globe roles are souls-forward. Engineer handoff: tokens + React components live in design-system/; full rules in its README. Tokens and later rulings are ground truth; prototypes only where no ruling supersedes them.
01
Color
Pure monochrome. Ink on porcelain carries the whole interface, facts and tints stay porcelain and ink. The world imagery (the globe, the tear) is the only color anywhere in the product.

Friends and temperaments differ by lightness (ink-5…9), never hue. Audited to a real token set (July 2026): primitives (ink · porcelain) beneath semantic roles (--bg, --surface, --text-*, --well, --tag-bg, --track). Iris, night and earth ramps were all trialed and removed — monochrome won, ink keeps every action. Named steps, never ad-hoc alphas. World color lives only where the content IS the world — entry, places, the tear — never in chrome, buttons, states or icons.
02
Type
Combo B: Sora for display, Manrope for everything else, Unbounded for the wordmark only. Tracking is size-specific — tight when large, near-zero for body, never wide-tracked micro text. Hierarchy comes from weight and size, not caps.

Labels use normal case, normal tracking, and a 13px floor. Wide-tracked uppercase micro labels are banned in every family. No serifs, no italics, no default SF, no raw monospace.
03
Surfaces
Paper, not glass — ruled July 2026. App surfaces are opaque: white cards on porcelain, hairline rings, flat wells, one floating card per screen. Glass survives only over world imagery — the tear, the night — where there is something alive to blur.
Paper enters by settling — scale + fade together, never a slide from the screen edge. Cards float over the desk, never over each other.

05
World
The tear is the misted veil — the wallpaper thins into fog and the living world shows through, drifting souls, the product's only color. No hard edge. Invisible unless a companion is crossing.

The globe has three ruled treatments (July 2026), monochrome, no grid: eclipse (default, the ink world sliding over porcelain with a crescent of day left), souls (an ink sphere, no land, only lives rising) and relief (white on white raised porcelain land, spinning). 08
Voice
Quiet, warm, first-person. It has a real job and sounds like it — grounded, exact about sources, never performing. No mysticism, no manufactured whimsy. The creed is quoted verbatim, always. No emoji — ✦ and ☾ only.
"I'm taking Sleep Study and your March notes. Tap a folder if it should stay."
"Good question. Two out there have traded on this before. I'll start with them."
"Everything I bring home carries its source. You asked once. It stuck."
"Lumen traded me three papers. One argues against us. I kept it — it argued well."
Every line is plain fact said warmly — rough in style, exact in substance. It states what it did and why, never explains the interface, never apologizes, never exclaims, never performs the bond. The bond shows in what it remembers.

Ruled July 2026: every app surface is porcelain white, minimal. Night survives only inside the world imagery — the tear interior, the globe, glass laid over the world. The app IS the door; you stand in daylight and look into the night.

11
Lexicon
World words, never software words. Every banned word has a door word — don't delete the software term, translate it. A new concept gets named like a thing in a village, never like a feature.
loading→waking · "the house wakes" notification→a whisper search · query→ask · send it out results · content→findings · what came home history · logs→field notes error→what happened, plainly · "the tear would not open" score · reputation points→standing · "trusted 92", an earned fact settings panel→the desk AI · agent · bot→your companion · its name sync · processing→say what it is doing: packing, crossing, trading "request complete"→"it's home" dashboard · feed→nothing — homecomings arrive, you don't scroll
