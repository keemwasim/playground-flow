the meeting

you are not looking at a listing. your one is meeting it. they face each other across the middle of the screen and the exchange happens between them, in their own words, while you watch. that is the door doctrine made literal, you are never inside the network, you are standing at it. it also gives the refusal somewhere honest to come from: it is not the app declining you, it is one creature reading another.
