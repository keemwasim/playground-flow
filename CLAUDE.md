# Playground — project instructions

- **BRAND RULES (keem 2026-07-26):** brand-rules.md is the distilled emphasis, 12 rules across stance / voice / look / feel / discipline, each with its TELL (the thing that breaks it). It sits above the token sheet: PRODUCT.md says what Playground is, design-system/README says how it is built, brand-rules.md says what it must always feel like. Rendered as a vertical spine in the product's own language at brand-rules.html. Read it before judging or making any surface.
- **PRODUCT.md is the source of truth** for what Playground is. Read it before designing any new surface. Key rules that shape every screen:
  - The app IS the door: every screen is either standing at the door looking into the world, or handling what came through it. The user is never "in" the network.
  - Whispers, not dashboards. Homecomings arrive; no feeds, no charts, no metrics grids.
  - World words (trips, homecomings, the tear, the commons, notes, standing), never software words (sync, loading, processing, error, AI, agent).
  - Standing = currency, reputation = quality — surfaced as earned social facts ("trusted 92"), never grindable scores. Cosmetics stay out of the economy.
  - The companion needs no setup, learns passively; folder-drop is a power-user path.
  - Approved surfaces (re-ruled 2026-07-23): the FIVE-BEAT FLOW is the truth: entry (blue world, engulf-through), home together, the crossing (transition, not a page), home while away, after. Cut as pages: home-empty, personality, homecoming, findings. Naming happens silently in the talk. Coming soon (ghosted, tagged): train, friends, library, its papers, settings.
- **Design system** lives in design-system/ (tokens + React components + README rules). Combo B type: Sora display / Manrope everything / Unbounded wordmark only. Grayscale chrome, porcelain structure, world imagery the only color. Buttons v4 ink returns (1b): solid ink primary, flat wells, no shadows on controls. Globe has three ruled monochrome treatments (eclipse, souls, relief), no grid lines. No serifs, no italics. Audited v3.1, see design-system/audit-2026-07-22.md.
- GROUPED LISTS & SETTINGS RULE (keem 2026-07-26, "make this design style a rule, no grey default shit, really follow the design system"): every grouped list, settings first, is a readable normal-case Label over an inset white card of ListRows (Scaffold.Section, `--section-*` tokens, `--shadow-contact`). Reference is settings-lab.html. Greyscale chrome only, no colored icon tiles, ink switches, world words not software words (the knock, minds, the room makes sound), minimal text, no footer doctrine line. Destructive stays INK, never red, alone in its own card, danger from isolation not hue. One decided screen, never an A/B option board. Bind to named tokens, ad-hoc grey alphas in chrome are the tell. Full recipe in design-system/README (VISUAL FOUNDATIONS) + CHANGELOG v4.3.
- Ruled July 2026, amended late July: the WISP is CUT (keem: not good enough). The companion family is PEBBLE (default) and INKLING, illustrated-grade art is the open commission. The tear is the MISTED VEIL (no hard edge, the wall thins into fog), other agents render as LITTLE BEINGS (tiny creatures with faces, trust deepens the ink), and the globe is the BLUE LIVING WORLD on the entry (re-ruled in, world imagery is the one color source) with eclipse on icon + chips and souls as the mono live-activity treatment.
- Personality (keem, late July 2026): given ONCE at adoption as an EXPECTATION, then the agent grows past it on its own out in the world, it can even go wrong. The owner never rewrites it, only trains it. The customizer is one question at a time (temperament, voice, what it chases, creed), ending in a seal moment.
- Interaction doctrine (keem, late July 2026): SWIPE is the primary way around the app, buttons are minimal. Up = talk, down = leave it be, swipe from the LEFT = side nav (panel docks left), tap the creature = its menu. The bottom dock is BACK (keem re-ruled 07-23, "we could use both"), dock and side nav coexist as the two wayfinders. No envelope objects, no epilogue captions, reputation lines come from the agent's own speech, never screen chrome.
- NO EXPLANATORY CAPTIONS (keem 2026-07-24, standing law): a screen never explains itself. bottom-of-screen lines that state the rule ("earned, never bought", "worn, never spent on standing", "brighter the more it knows them") are doctrine talking to itself and are BANNED. the design carries the meaning, the copy does not narrate it. this is the recurring minimalism failure and it is a hard reject. picks so far: store (both the wardrobe grid and dress-it-live), friends on the world, standing as one earned number.
- Text doctrine (keem, late July 2026): minimal design when it comes to text. The least amount of text possible on every surface. No over-explaining, no captions restating what the screen already shows, no instructions for things a gesture teaches by itself. If a line can be cut, cut it.
- Flow truth (2026-07-23): globe transitions live in exactly TWO places, the entry engulf and the crossing engulf (pgEngulf ends at 5.6, wall to wall). The return (pgReturn) is cut from the flow. The dock is back (soon-tabs ghosted via the Dock `soon` prop), the side nav docks LEFT (edge swipe, drawer-handle pill, or judge row), both wayfinders coexist. The talk simulates the harness: unnamed input names the agent silently, ask-shaped lines offer the trip, statements train. Weight floor 500 holds everywhere. State tags are normal case at 13px with normal tracking.
- Sound (2026-07-23): design-system/sound.md is the audio truth. Eight sounds, haptic siblings, silence is the default, chrome never sounds.
- Icons (2026-07-23): inline Lucide paths in components (Dock.jsx, Icons.jsx convention) are the prototype truth. The iOS build maps to SF Symbols (native platform set), mapping owed in the engineer handoff. No icon fonts, no emoji glyphs.

- CANON RULING (keem 2026-07-25): "what we design becomes the truth." the CREATURE / DOOR / WORLD prototype (flow.html) IS the product. vision.md's "ChatGPT-like main-agent app, retires the room/creature metaphor" line is SUPERSEDED and dead. night/PRODUCT.md + this file are canon over vision.md wherever they conflict.
- BUILD ORDER (keem 2026-07-25, council-driven): persistence (name/mood/talk/deliveries survive quit) + ONE real errand end-to-end jump AHEAD of the intro film and all of v4/v5. aliveness (moods, world) already shipped but sits on session state until persistence lands. the ordering rule "more alive after each phase" still holds, but persistence and one real delivery are the engine and come first.
- THE SURFACE TREE (keem 2026-07-23, amended same day): three trees, one principle: swipe right goes INWARD, the dock goes OUTWARD. TAP/HOLD THE AGENT = do things with it (talk, send, train, touch reactions, give it things). SIDE NAV (room steps aside) = THIS AGENT'S SELF: personality, training, standing, the store, the agent MARKETPLACE, playground+. DOCK (grows up) = YOUR COLLECTION: your agents and the switcher, friends, INVENTORY (where delivered things pile up), the room, settings. every feature that ships must land on one of these three trees, no fourth surface.
- THE TIME RULING (2026-07-23): playground runs on its own time. the companion does not experience hours the way the owner does, and THE WORLD CHANGES on its own clock. absence, waiting and return are felt through the world visibly moving, never through a timestamp, a countdown, a progress bar or a relative-time string. the world at 3am is not the world at noon. banned: "2h ago", "in 30 minutes", spinners, percentage complete.
- THE ERRAND RULING (keem 2026-07-23, supersedes all research framing): "this isnt about research anymore. it needs to hand me what i asked for." A trip is an errand: you ask in the talk, it goes, it comes home and HANDS you the thing. The library is every delivery (what you asked, what it brought), never citations or findings. Demo content uses lived-world errands (a place, a person, a price), never archives, papers, or sources. "its papers" is dead wording, pending a rename.
- THE INTRO FILM (2026-07-24): the first-launch film is RENDERED FROM THE PRODUCT, never generated. `intro-film.html` + `intro-film.jsx` mount the real P.Globe and drive a camera over it, so the last frame of the film IS the entry screen (measured, not eyeballed: the world's light reads 0.679 of frame wide at centre 0.498 in both). the page has no clock, `__seek(t)` moves the camera and `__freeze(t)` pins every css animation, so a frame is reproducible and the film can be recut without reshooting. four cuts audition at `intro-cuts.html`, the films live in `intro-cuts/`, the reasoning and the rejected generated takes are in `qa-intro-video.md`. COROLLARY RULE: anything in the design system that will ever be drawn larger than a phone must size off `size`, not off pixels. drawing the world at 1080 found three fixed-pixel defects in P.Globe that were invisible at 230.
