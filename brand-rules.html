brand rules

the handful of things every surface has been quietly insisting on. not the token sheet, the higher altitude. each rule carries its tell, the thing that breaks it, because the brand is defended at the tell.
the stance
1
the app is the door
you stand at the door looking in, you are never inside the network. every screen is either at the door or handling what came through it.
tella screen that puts the user "in" the world, a network the user operates.
2
a bond, not a dashboard
homecomings arrive, they are not fetched. no feeds, no charts, no metrics, no score to grind. the bond shows in what it remembers, never in a speech about it.
tella timeline to scroll, a stat card about the companion, a progress meter.
the voice
3
world words, never software words
trips, homecomings, the tear, the commons, minds, the knock, standing. banned: AI, agent, model, sync, loading, error, dashboard, feed, score.
tell"notifications" not "the knock", "AI model" not "minds", "sync failed".
4
the least text that carries it
a screen never explains itself. no caption restating what the screen shows, no instruction for what a gesture teaches, no footer doctrine line. if a line can be cut, cut it.
tella sentence under a control telling you what it does or what the rule is.
5
the companion speaks, quietly
first person, warm, brief, rough in style and exact in substance, never performing, never mystical. it is yours the way a pet is. its creed is quoted back verbatim.
tellan assistant tone, an exclamation, an apology twice, whimsy for its own sake.
the look
6
greyscale chrome, the world is the only color
pure ink on porcelain. saturated color appears only where the content is the world, the tear interior and world imagery. never in chrome, buttons, states, icons, or a destructive row.
tella colored icon tile, a red delete, a blue toggle, an accent anywhere in chrome.
7
flat controls, depth is paper and glass
solid ink primary, flat wells, no shadows on controls. paper gets a hairline ring plus a contact shadow. glass survives only over world imagery, where something alive can blur.
tella raised gradient button, an inset ring, glass on app chrome, a bevel.
8
one type voice
Sora for display titles, Manrope for everything else, Unbounded for the wordmark only. weight never below 500. no serifs, no italics, no system font.
tella 400 body line, an italic, a second display face sneaking in.
the feel
9
the world runs on its own time
no clock, no countdown, no percentage, no spinner, no "2h ago". absence, waiting and return are felt through the world visibly moving. the world at 3am is not the world at noon.
tella relative-time string, a progress percentage, a loading spinner.
10
swipe first, buttons last
swipe is the primary way around. up is talk, down is leave it be, from the left is the self, tap the creature is its menu, the dock is back. buttons are minimal.
tella screen navigated only by buttons, a tap where a swipe should carry it.
the discipline
11
one decided screen, never an option board
ship the decision, not an A/B for keem to ratify. exploration happens before the screen, not on it.
telltwo takes side by side with a dot to pick, a screen that asks to be chosen.
12
named tokens, never ad-hoc grey
every value binds to a system role: divider, well, paper-ring, the shadow scale. ad-hoc grey alphas in chrome are the tell the system was not followed.
tellrgba(0,0,0,.14) where the named toggle-off token was meant. grey default shit.
