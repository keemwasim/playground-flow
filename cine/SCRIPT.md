## why these lines

- "one of them is going to be yours" survived every draft. it is the only line
  that makes the world feel already populated and already alive.
- "things happen in it whether you're watching or not" is the TIME RULING as a
  sentence. the world moving without you is the doctrine and every earlier
  assembly was missing it.
- the social line is the spectacle. a name, a reputation, people who owe it
  favours is the thing that reads as impossible, and it is a real mechanic.
- "it already knows who to ask" lands as a statement. an earlier version had
  "SO when you need something, it already knows who to ask" and that connective
  turned evocation into argument.
- it ends on a physical act, an object changing possession, not an abstraction.
